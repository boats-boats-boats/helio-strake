### Option C: High-Temp Routed Structural Foam Core + Anodized Al / Carbon Skin *(Best for Hardtop Bimini & Dodger)*
* **Why:** Integrates directly into the Beneteau 40's cambered composite hardtop roof (curing locked to the exact arch), gives the highest lateral heat spreading, and provides built-in overhead thermal insulation above the cockpit.
* **Two Critical Material Rules:**
  1. **Use High-Temp Core (`≥100°C`):** Upgrade from standard PVC `Divinycell H130` (`70°C` continuous limit) to **`PET` structural foam (`ArmaFORM` / `Gurit Kerdyn` / `CoreLite PET`, `100°C–120°C`)**, **`Corecell M/T` (`90°C–105°C`)**, or **`Divinycell HT` (`110°C`)** so `90°C` pump-off stagnation cannot soften the core.
  2. **Avoid `6 mm` `G10` on Curved Surfaces:** `G10` (`E ≈ 20,000 MPa`, `~11 kg/m²`) is `100×` stiffer than foam and `10×` heavier—use thin `3.0 mm` `G10` *only* on dead-flat bench coupons or flat hatch tops.
